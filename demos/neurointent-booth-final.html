NeuroIntent

Same words same score.

A rehearsal booth for interview answers. NeuroIntent scores what you said from the transcript alone, and keeps your pitch, pace and pauses out of that score.

Start a rehearsal Scroll to take an answer apart
i.

Every answer arrives as one signal

In a recording, what you said and how you said it are tangled together. Most automated interview tools score the whole tangle, so a flat pitch or a long pause can cost points that have nothing to do with the answer.

ii.

Pull it into two

Whisper writes the words down and RoBERTa reads them. openSMILE takes 62 measurements of the voice. The model is trained to keep the two representations statistically independent, so the two sit at right angles, like two walls of a corner.
